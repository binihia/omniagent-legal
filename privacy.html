Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

OmniAgent ("the Software") is a private, self-hosted tool operated by a single individual ("the Operator"). It is used only by the Operator to manage and publish their own content to their own connected social-media accounts. It has no other users and provides no service to third parties.

1. Data the Software accesses

When the Operator connects one of their own accounts through a platform's official OAuth flow, the Software may access, for that account only:

  • Basic profile information (such as account name, username, avatar and account ID) used to identify the connected account in the Operator's own dashboard.
  • Permission to upload or post video content that the Operator explicitly chooses to publish.

The Software does not read private messages, does not collect data about other users, and does not browse or scrape content.

2. How data is stored

All data is stored locally on the Operator's own machine. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Nothing is stored on any third-party server operated by the Software's authors.

3. How data is used

Access tokens are used solely to display the connected account and to publish content that the Operator initiates. Data is not sold, rented, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Data retention and deletion

Tokens and account references are kept until the Operator disconnects the account or deletes the local data store, at which point they are removed. The Operator can also revoke the Software's access at any time from the security settings of the relevant platform.

5. Third-party platforms

Use of each connected platform is governed by that platform's own privacy policy (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram/Facebook). This policy covers only the Software itself.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
